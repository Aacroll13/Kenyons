Benchmarks loaded. In-house costs from 2026 artisan soap cost surveys (ingredients ~$0.77/bar, labour $1.24/bar, packaging $0.32/bar, true cost ≈ $2.33/bar); private-label base bars $0.50–1.20 at MOQ with custom stamping ~+$0.25; MOQs 500 bars (paper sleeve) to 3,000 (boxed), ~1,000 for a custom formula. Quality cold-process soap retails $10–18/bar in 2026. Standard convention is cost × 2.35 = wholesale, wholesale × 2 = retail.

Regulatory reminder. True soap — saponified oils sold solely for cleansing — sits outside FDA cosmetic regulation and MoCRA. Claiming the bar moisturises, exfoliates or soothes makes it a cosmetic; a therapeutic claim can make it an unapproved drug. Nothing in this calculator changes that: price and margin are business questions, label wording is a legal one. Sell the mill, the stones and the corn — not what the soap does to skin.

Not legal or financial advice. Verify costs with actual supplier quotes and label language with a regulatory specialist or the Handcrafted Soap & Cosmetic Guild before launch.
